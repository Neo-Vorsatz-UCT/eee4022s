\chapter{Security Standard}\label{ch:sec_std}

The security of the teleoperation system can be quantified by checking how many of the requirements are met for the OWASP IoT Security Verification Standard (ISVS) 1.0.0-RC2 \cite{bib:OWASP_ISVS}, which is currently under review for publishing. \autoref{tab_sec_std:isvs} shows the level 1 requirements from the standard, whether they're passed for the teleoperation system, and what kind of attacks or vulnerabilities the standard protects against.

\begin{table}[h!]
\centering
\caption{Teleoperation Compared to OWASP ISVS 1.0.0-RC2}
\label{tab_sec_std:isvs}
\small{
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|p{0.5\linewidth}|}
    \hline
    \textbf{ID} & \textbf{Pass/Fail} & \textbf{Attacks} \\
    \hline \hline
    1.1.1 & - & - \\
    1.1.2 & True & Unmaintained entryways \\
    1.2.3 & True & Undocumented security protocols \\
    1.2.4 & False & Privilege escalation on same device \\
    1.2.5 & False & Compromised server-side components \\
    
\end{tabular}
}
\end{table}
